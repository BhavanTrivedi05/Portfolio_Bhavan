Cosmos

Aug 2026 — now
Motorsport news and community platform

A news reader for motorsport across every series, with reaction-based discussion on stories. I designed the schema, wrote the service, and deployed it to a free-tier ARM machine where it runs under systemd behind Caddy. Live commenting, car and tech explainers, and a wider platform are what I'm building toward.

It grew out of an older codebase of mine, which I audited before building anything on top of it. Eight security defects: a credential committed to the repo, a path to taking over an admin account, passwords stored in plaintext, and a rate limiter that never limited anything. I fixed them one at a time with an AI coding agent driving, checking every change against the test suite.

I also measured what running in a container would cost me in memory against the RAM the free tier gives me, and ran the service under systemd instead of defaulting to Docker.

Student Survival Kit

Spring 2026
Desktop app for international students, database coursework

Visa documents, academic deadlines, job applications, expenses, housing leases, courses, contacts, and health records in one place — the logistics that otherwise live in eight different apps. It ships with a Swing interface and a command-line one over the same code.

The work worth talking about is underneath. Ten normalized tables with foreign keys cascading on delete and update, three stored procedures for expiring documents, upcoming deadlines, and monthly expense totals, a dashboard view, and a trigger that marks a deadline missed the moment you enter one that's already past. Every query goes through a prepared statement, and the whole schema installs from one script you can run twice.
